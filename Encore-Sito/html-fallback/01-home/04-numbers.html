Regulation (EU) 2024/1781 (Ecodesign for Sustainable Products Regulation), Article 25. Medium-sized companies from 2030.

Arena di Verona: up to 594,000 tonnes a year, baled at 450 kg per cubic metre (ISO 8115), take about 1.3 million cubic metres. The Arena, filled to the top of its 24 m walls, holds about 285,000 cubic metres. That is 4.6 times, rounded down to four.
